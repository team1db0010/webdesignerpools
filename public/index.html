About Designer Pools

Three generations of pool building excellence

Designer Pools by Garry Bair, LLC is a family-owned swimming pool construction company with deep roots in the craft. Our story began long before our name—back in the 1970s, when Floyd Bair helped introduce shotcrete techniques to the Kansas City area and passed the trade down to his son, Garry.

After years of hands-on experience as a subcontractor and industry innovator—including designing the Minitower MT-20 manhole sprayer—Garry founded Designer Pools in 2004 with a simple goal: build pools the right way, with pride in the details.

Following Garry's unexpected passing in December 2023, his son Derek Bair stepped into the role of Managing Partner in January 2024, continuing the family tradition as a third-generation pool builder. Derek brings more than 20 years of experience in the pool industry and learned the craft directly from Garry, who passed down not only his knowledge but also his guiding mantra: "hard work, honesty, and integrity."

Working alongside Derek is Colin Barker, Managing Partner and Project Manager. Colin spent the past decade working directly with Garry and Derek, learning the trade on job sites throughout the Kansas City area. Today, Colin serves as the driving force behind day-to-day operations in the field, ensuring every project is executed with the same standards of craftsmanship and attention to detail that Garry demanded.

Whether you're envisioning a classic backyard retreat or a fully customized outdoor living environment, every project we build reflects the values that shaped our company from the beginning—integrity, clear communication, and durable construction built to last.
